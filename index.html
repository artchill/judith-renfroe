Goodnight Honey Bear

ISBN-13: 979-8895314425

"The book that I have told you about, Goodnight Honey Bear, was inspired by a flight from Fort Myers, Florida, to Pittsburgh. I had heard music which reminded me of my mother, and therefore reminded me of my daughter Heather, and how she would always say to her youngest, ‘Good night, honey bear.’ I wrote that book on the 2½-hour flight, and I'm happy I did — it means a lot to me."

Meet the Author

Judith Renfroe

I am Judith Renfroe. I live in Key West, Florida — a place where the moon hangs low over the water and the days are full of small wonders.

I enjoy writing, and I look forward to having several books published in the near future. My stories grow out of the people I love most: my children, my grandchildren, and the quiet moments we share — at bedtime, by the shore, anywhere love finds room to breathe.

"Loving, caring, giving, sharing — and spreading love and good wishes everywhere I go."
